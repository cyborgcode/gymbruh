# Month 9: Powerbuilding

**Weeks 35-38** · Quarter 3: *Build the Look: Hypertrophy & Aesthetics* · 3 sessions/week

[← Month 8](08-aesthetic-specialization.md) · [Overview](../README.md) · [Month 10 →](10-strength-peak.md)

## Goal

Strength and size together: one heavy top set followed by volume back-off sets. This bridges the aesthetics block into the strength peak.

## This month at a glance

| | Prescription |
|---|---|
| **Main lifts** | See the week-by-week table · rest 3 min (top set), 2 min (back-offs) |
| **Compound accessories** | 3×8-10 @ RIR 1 · rest 90 s |
| **Isolation** | 3×12-15 @ RIR 1 · rest 60 s |
| **Abs** | 2 exercises every session, done as a superset (see each day) |
| **Warm-up** | 10 min before every session: easy bike or rower, mobility, ramp-up sets ([details](../README.md#warm-up-10-min)) |
| **Intensity technique** | Drop set on the last set of every isolation exercise. |
| **Nutrition** | Lean gain: TDEE + 200-300 kcal, protein 1.6-2.2 g/kg. Starting with higher body fat? Eat at maintenance (recomposition). |

## Main lifts: week by week

Applies to all six main lifts: **Bench Press, Overhead Press** (Push), **Barbell Row, Pull-up** (Pull), **Leg Press, Hip Thrust** (Legs).

| Week | Sets × Reps | Load (% of 1RM) | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (wk 35) | 1×5 then 3×8 | 82.5% then 70% | Top set RPE 8 |
| 2 (wk 36) | 1×4 then 3×7 | 85% then 72.5% | Top set RPE 8 |
| 3 (wk 37) | 1×3 then 3×6 | 87.5% then 75% | Top set RPE 8 |
| 4 (wk 38) | 1×2 then 3×5 | 90% then 77.5% | Top set RPE 8.5 |

> **Load basis:** Percentages are of the Week 25 1RM. If the top set feels RPE 7 or easier, add 2.5% next week.

## Day A: Push (chest, shoulders, triceps)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Barbell Bench Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 3 min (top set), 2 min (back-offs) | Shoulder blades squeezed and down, feet planted, bar to mid-chest, press up and slightly back. |
| 2 | **Standing Overhead Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 3 min (top set), 2 min (back-offs) | Squeeze glutes and abs, bar travels straight up, push your head through at lockout. |
| 3 | Incline Dumbbell Press (30°) | 3×8-10 | RIR 1 | 90 s | Upper-chest priority. Deep stretch, 3 s on the way down. |
| 4a | Cable Y-Raise | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Cable low, sweep the arm up in a Y in front of the body. Targets the side delts. |
| 4b | Overhead Rope Triceps Extension | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Face away from the stack, deep stretch. |
| 5a | Low-to-High Cable Fly | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Cables low, sweep the hands up to chin height. Targets the upper chest. |
| 5b | Single-Arm Cable Pushdown | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Reverse or neutral grip, full lockout. |
| 5c | Lean-Away Cable Lateral Raise | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Hold the post and lean away. Keeps tension on the delt at the bottom. |
| 6a | Abs: Toes-to-Bar (or Hanging Leg Raise) | 3×10 | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Controlled. Use the lats to stay still and curl the pelvis up. |
| 6b | Abs: Weighted Decline Crunch | 4×10 | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Plate on your chest, crunch, don't sit all the way up. |

## Day B: Pull (back, rear delts, biceps)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Barbell Row** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 3 min (top set), 2 min (back-offs) | Unrack from safeties at knee height so you never pull the bar off the floor. Torso 30-45°, brace, pull to the lower ribs. |
| 2 | **Weighted Pull-up** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 3 min (top set), 2 min (back-offs) | Load with a dip belt. The % covers bodyweight + added weight (see the pull-up formula in the README). |
| 3 | Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown | 3×8-10 | RIR 1 | 90 s | Width builder. Pull the elbows down and out, squeeze the lats at the bottom. |
| 4a | Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Arms nearly straight, sweep the bar down to your thighs. Isolates the lats for width. |
| 4b | Bayesian Cable Curl | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Face away from a low cable with the arm behind the body for a full stretch. |
| 5a | Rear-Delt Cable Fly | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Cross the cables, sweep the arms wide at shoulder height. |
| 5b | Preacher Curl | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Full stretch at the bottom, don't let the elbows lift. |
| 6a | Abs: Ab Wheel Rollout (from feet, shortened range) | 3×6-8 | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Roll toward a wall or up an incline to limit the range. Not ready? Kneeling with a 3 s pause. |
| 6b | Abs: Cable Woodchopper (high to low) | 3×15/side | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Rotate through the trunk, arms stay long. |

## Day C: Legs (quads, glutes, hamstrings, calves)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Leg Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 3 min (top set), 2 min (back-offs) | Feet shoulder-width, mid-platform. Lower until your hips are about to lift off the pad. Never lock the knees. |
| 2 | **Barbell Hip Thrust** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 3 min (top set), 2 min (back-offs) | Upper back on the bench, chin tucked, ribs down. Shins vertical at lockout, 1 s squeeze at the top. |
| 3 | High-Box Dumbbell Step-up | 3×8-10 | RIR 1 | 90 s | Box a bit above knee height for more glute. Control the way down. |
| 4a | Leg Extension | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | 1 s squeeze at the top, 3 s on the way down. |
| 4b | Lying Leg Curl | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Press your hips into the pad, 3 s on the way down. |
| 4c | Seated Calf Raise | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | 2 s pause at the bottom, 1 s squeeze at the top. |
| 5a | 45° Back Extension | 3×8-10 | RIR 1 | 90 s after the superset | Round slightly over the pad and squeeze the glutes to rise. Hold a plate once 3×12 is easy. |
| 5b | Seated Leg Curl | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 90 s after the superset | Lean your torso forward a little for a bigger stretch. |
| 5c | Standing Calf Raise | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 90 s after the superset | 2 s pause in the full stretch at the bottom. |
| 6a | Abs: Dragon Flag Negative | 3×5 (5 s down) | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Grip the bench behind your head, body rigid, lower over 5 s. |
| 6b | Abs: Hollow Body Hold | 3×40 s | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Lower back pressed into the floor, arms and legs long. |

> Exercises with the same number and a letter (4a/4b/4c, 5a/5b/5c) are a **superset**: do one set of each back to back, then rest.

## Flex week (Week 39)

Every quarter ends with one flex week. Choose **one** option:

1. **Deload (recommended):** 3 easy sessions: main lifts **2×5 at 50-60%**, 1 easy set of each accessory, and 2 sets of each abs exercise.
2. **Catch-up:** if you missed sessions this quarter, make them up now.
3. **Full rest or holiday:** no gym, but keep walking 8-10k steps a day.

Before Month 10, compare your Week 25 test with the e1RM from your Month 9, Week 4 top sets, and use the **higher** number.
