# Month 7: Hypertrophy III: Volume

**Weeks 27-30** · Quarter 3: *Build the Look: Hypertrophy & Aesthetics* · 3 sessions/week

[← Month 6](06-strength-realization-test.md) · [Overview](../README.md) · [Month 8 →](08-aesthetic-specialization.md)

## Goal

Use your new strength to build muscle: higher volume and slow eccentrics. The accessory list shifts toward V-taper aesthetics (upper chest, side delts, lats, arms).

## This month at a glance

| | Prescription |
|---|---|
| **Main lifts** | See the week-by-week table · rest 2 min |
| **Compound accessories** | 4×10-12 @ RIR 1-2 · rest 90 s |
| **Isolation** | 3×12-15 @ RIR 1 · rest 60 s |
| **Light block** (superset 5) | The muscles you train hard next session: same reps, at most 3 sets, RIR 2-3 |
| **Abs** | 2 exercises every session, done as a superset (see each day) |
| **Warm-up** | 10 min before every session: easy bike or rower, mobility, ramp-up sets ([details](../README.md#warm-up-10-min)) |
| **Intensity technique** | 3 s lowering on every accessory rep. On the last set of isolation work, finish with lengthened partials (half reps in the stretched position) until RIR 0. The light block (superset 5) gets no finishers: stop 2-3 reps short there. |
| **Nutrition** | Lean gain: TDEE + 200-300 kcal, protein 1.6-2.2 g/kg. Starting with higher body fat? Eat at maintenance (recomposition). |

## Main lifts: week by week

Applies to all six main lifts: **Bench Press, Overhead Press** (Push), **Pull-up, Barbell Row** (Pull), **Leg Press, Hip Thrust** (Legs).

| Week | Sets × Reps | Load (% of 1RM) | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (wk 27) | 4×10 | 67.5% | RIR 2 |
| 2 (wk 28) | 4×9 | 70% | RIR 2 |
| 3 (wk 29) | 4×8 | 72.5% | RIR 1-2 |
| 4 (wk 30) | 4×8 | 75% | RIR 1 |

> **Load basis:** Percentages are of the 1RM you tested in Week 25.

## Day A: Push + light Pull (chest, shoulders, triceps · light block: back, biceps)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Barbell Bench Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Shoulder blades squeezed and down, feet planted, bar to mid-chest, press up and slightly back. |
| 2 | **Standing Overhead Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Squeeze glutes and abs, bar travels straight up, push your head through at lockout. |
| 3 | Incline Dumbbell Press (30°) | 4×10-12 | RIR 1-2 | 90 s | Upper-chest priority. Deep stretch, 3 s on the way down. |
| 4a | Cable Y-Raise | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Cable low, sweep the arm up in a Y in front of the body. Targets the side delts. |
| 4b | Overhead Rope Triceps Extension | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Face away from the stack, deep stretch. |
| 5a | Light: Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown | 3×12-15 | RIR 2-3 | 60 s after the superset | Arms nearly straight, sweep the bar down to your thighs. Isolates the lats for width. |
| 5b | Light: Bayesian Cable Curl | 3×12-15 | RIR 2-3 | 60 s after the superset | Face away from a low cable with the arm behind the body for a full stretch. |
| 6a | Abs: Toes-to-Bar (or Hanging Leg Raise) | 3×6 | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Controlled. Use the lats to stay still and curl the pelvis up. |
| 6b | Abs: Weighted Decline Crunch | 3×10 | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Plate on your chest, crunch, don't sit all the way up. |

## Day B: Pull + light Legs (back, rear delts, biceps · light block: quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Weighted Pull-up** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Load with a dip belt. The % covers bodyweight + added weight (see the pull-up formula in the README). |
| 2 | **Barbell Row** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Unrack from safeties at knee height so you never pull the bar off the floor. Torso 30-45°, brace, pull to the lower ribs. |
| 3 | Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown | 4×10-12 | RIR 1-2 | 90 s | Width builder. Pull the elbows down and out, squeeze the lats at the bottom. |
| 4a | Rear-Delt Cable Fly | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Cross the cables, sweep the arms wide at shoulder height. |
| 4b | Preacher Curl | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Full stretch at the bottom, don't let the elbows lift. |
| 5a | Light: Leg Extension | 3×12-15 | RIR 2-3 | 90 s after the superset | 1 s squeeze at the top, 3 s on the way down. |
| 5b | Light: 45° Back Extension | 3×10-12 | RIR 2-3 | 90 s after the superset | Hinge at the hips until you feel the hamstrings stretch, then squeeze the glutes to come up. Stop when your body is straight. Hold a plate once 12 reps are easy. |
| 5c | Light: Seated Calf Raise | 3×12-15 | RIR 2-3 | 90 s after the superset | 2 s pause at the bottom, 1 s squeeze at the top. |
| 6a | Abs: Ab Wheel Rollout (from feet, shortened range) | 3×4-5 | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Roll toward a wall or up an incline to limit the range. Not ready? Kneeling with a 3 s pause. |
| 6b | Abs: Cable Woodchopper (high to low) | 3×12/side | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Rotate through the trunk, arms stay long. |

## Day C: Legs + light Push (quads, glutes, hamstrings, calves · light block: chest, side delts, triceps)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Leg Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Feet shoulder-width, mid-platform. Lower until your hips are about to lift off the pad. Never lock the knees. |
| 2 | **Barbell Hip Thrust** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Upper back on the bench, chin tucked, ribs down. Shins vertical at lockout, 1 s squeeze at the top. |
| 3 | Bulgarian Split Squat (dumbbells) | 4×10-12 | RIR 1-2 | 90 s | Back foot on a bench, weight on the front leg. Lower until the back knee nearly touches the floor. Lean forward a little for more glutes. |
| 4a | Seated Leg Curl | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Lean your torso forward a little for a bigger stretch. |
| 4b | Standing Calf Raise | 3×12-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | 2 s pause in the full stretch at the bottom. |
| 5a | Light: Low-to-High Cable Fly | 3×12-15 | RIR 2-3 | 60 s after the superset | Cables low, sweep the hands up to chin height. Targets the upper chest. |
| 5b | Light: Lean-Away Cable Lateral Raise | 3×12-15 | RIR 2-3 | 60 s after the superset | Hold the post and lean away. Keeps tension on the delt at the bottom. |
| 5c | Light: Single-Arm Cable Pushdown | 3×12-15 | RIR 2-3 | 60 s after the superset | Reverse or neutral grip, full lockout. |
| 6a | Abs: Dragon Flag Negative | 3×3 (5 s down) | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Grip the bench behind your head, body rigid, lower over 5 s. |
| 6b | Abs: Hollow Body Hold | 3×20 s | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Lower back pressed into the floor, arms and legs long. |

> Exercises with the same number and a letter (4a/4b, 5a/5b/5c) are a **superset**: do one set of each back to back, then rest. Superset 5 is the **light block**: it trains the muscles you work hard next session, so stop 2-3 reps short.
