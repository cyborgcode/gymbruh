# Month 5: Strength II: Intensification

**Weeks 18-21** · Quarter 2: *Get Strong: Strength* · 3 sessions/week

[← Month 4](04-strength-accumulation.md) · [Overview](../README.md) · [Month 6 →](06-strength-realization-test.md)

## Goal

Heavier weights, fewer reps (3-4). This is where the big strength gains come from. Week 4 is a deload.

## This month at a glance

| | Prescription |
|---|---|
| **Main lifts** | See the week-by-week table · rest 3 min |
| **Compound accessories** | 3×6-8 @ RIR 2 · rest 2 min |
| **Isolation** | 3×10-12 @ RIR 1-2 · rest 60-75 s |
| **Abs** | 2 exercises every session, done as a superset (see each day) |
| **Cardio** | 30 min before every session: Zone 2, low-impact only. |
| **Intensity technique** | None. |
| **Nutrition** | Lean gain: TDEE + 200-300 kcal, protein 1.6-2.2 g/kg. Starting with higher body fat? Eat at maintenance (recomposition). |

## Main lifts: week by week

Applies to all six main lifts: **Bench Press, Leg Press, Hip Thrust, Barbell Row, Overhead Press, Pull-up**.

| Week | Sets × Reps | Load (% of 1RM) | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (wk 18) | 5×4 | 82.5% | RIR 2 |
| 2 (wk 19) | 5×3 | 85% | RIR 2 |
| 3 (wk 20) | 4×3 | 87.5% | RIR 1-2 |
| 4 (wk 21) | 3×3 | 70% | RIR 4+ (deload) |

> **Load basis:** Percentages are of your Month 3 e1RM. If Month 4 felt easy, add 2.5% to the e1RM (autoregulation rule, README).

> Exercises with the same number and a letter (3a/3b, 5a/5b/5c) are a **superset**: do one set of each back to back, then rest.

### Week 4: Deload

- **Main lifts:** follow the Week 4 row in the main-lift table.
- **Everything else:** cut sets in half (usually to **2 sets**). Keep the same reps, drop the load 10-15%, and stop at **RIR 4 or more**.
- **Abs:** 2 sets each.
- **Cardio:** the full 30 min, but easy Zone 2 only.
- The point of a deload is to clear fatigue so you come back stronger. Don't skip it, and don't turn it into a hard week.
