# Month 8: Aesthetic Specialization (V-Taper)

**Weeks 31-34** · Quarter 3: *Build the Look: Hypertrophy & Aesthetics* · 3 sessions/week

[← Month 7](07-hypertrophy-volume.md) · [Overview](../README.md) · [Month 9 →](09-powerbuilding.md)

## Goal

Specialization month for the V-taper look: extra sets for side delts, upper chest and lats, plus intensity techniques. The main lifts drop to 3×6, which is enough to keep your strength.

## This month at a glance

| | Prescription |
|---|---|
| **Main lifts** | See the week-by-week table · rest 2-3 min |
| **Compound accessories** | 3×8-12 @ RIR 0-1 · rest 90 s |
| **Isolation** | 3×10-15 @ RIR 0-1 · rest 60 s |
| **Abs** | 2 exercises every session, done as a superset (see each day) |
| **Warm-up** | 10 min before every session: easy bike or rower, mobility, ramp-up sets ([details](../README.md#warm-up-10-min)) |
| **Intensity technique** | ★ = specialization exercise and gets +1 set (already included below). Rest-pause on the last set of compound accessories (rest 15 s, rep out again, twice). Myo-reps on the last set of isolation work (one set to RIR 1, then 3-4 mini-sets of 3-5 reps with 15 s rest). |
| **Nutrition** | Lean gain: TDEE + 200-300 kcal, protein 1.6-2.2 g/kg. Starting with higher body fat? Eat at maintenance (recomposition). |

## Main lifts: week by week

Applies to all six main lifts: **Bench Press, Overhead Press** (Push), **Barbell Row, Pull-up** (Pull), **Leg Press, Hip Thrust** (Legs).

| Week | Sets × Reps | Load (% of 1RM) | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (wk 31) | 3×6 | 75% | RIR 3 |
| 2 (wk 32) | 3×6 | 77.5% | RIR 2-3 |
| 3 (wk 33) | 3×6 | 80% | RIR 2 |
| 4 (wk 34) | 2×6 | 65% | RIR 4+ (deload) |

> **Load basis:** Percentages are of the 1RM you tested in Week 25.

| 2 | **Standing Overhead Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2-3 min | Squeeze glutes and abs, bar travels straight up, push your head through at lockout. |

| 4b | Overhead Rope Triceps Extension | 3×10-15 | RIR 0-1 | 60 s after the superset | Face away from the stack, deep stretch. |

## Day B: Pull (back, rear delts, biceps)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Barbell Row** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2-3 min | Unrack from safeties at knee height so you never pull the bar off the floor. Torso 30-45°, brace, pull to the lower ribs. |
| 2 | **Weighted Pull-up** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2-3 min | Load with a dip belt. The % covers bodyweight + added weight (see the pull-up formula in the README). |
| 3 | Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown ★ | 4×8-12 | RIR 0-1 | 90 s | Width builder. Pull the elbows down and out, squeeze the lats at the bottom. |
| 4a | Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown ★ | 4×10-15 | RIR 0-1 | 60 s after the superset | Arms nearly straight, sweep the bar down to your thighs. Isolates the lats for width. |
| 4b | Bayesian Cable Curl | 3×10-15 | RIR 0-1 | 60 s after the superset | Face away from a low cable with the arm behind the body for a full stretch. |
| 5a | Rear-Delt Cable Fly | 3×10-15 | RIR 0-1 | 60 s after the superset | Cross the cables, sweep the arms wide at shoulder height. |
| 5b | Preacher Curl | 3×10-15 | RIR 0-1 | 60 s after the superset | Full stretch at the bottom, don't let the elbows lift. |
| 6a | Abs: Ab Wheel Rollout (from feet, shortened range) | 3×5-6 | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Roll toward a wall or up an incline to limit the range. Not ready? Kneeling with a 3 s pause. |
| 6b | Abs: Cable Woodchopper (high to low) | 3×12/side (heavier) | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Rotate through the trunk, arms stay long. |

## Day C: Legs (quads, glutes, hamstrings, calves)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Leg Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2-3 min | Feet shoulder-width, mid-platform. Lower until your hips are about to lift off the pad. Never lock the knees. |
| 2 | **Barbell Hip Thrust** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2-3 min | Upper back on the bench, chin tucked, ribs down. Shins vertical at lockout, 1 s squeeze at the top. |
| 3 | High-Box Dumbbell Step-up | 3×8-12 | RIR 0-1 | 90 s | Box a bit above knee height for more glute. Control the way down. |
| 4a | Leg Extension | 3×10-15 | RIR 0-1 | 60 s after the superset | 1 s squeeze at the top, 3 s on the way down. |
| 4b | Lying Leg Curl | 3×10-15 | RIR 0-1 | 60 s after the superset | Press your hips into the pad, 3 s on the way down. |
| 4c | Seated Calf Raise | 3×10-15 | RIR 0-1 | 60 s after the superset | 2 s pause at the bottom, 1 s squeeze at the top. |
| 5a | 45° Back Extension | 3×8-12 | RIR 0-1 | 90 s after the superset | Round slightly over the pad and squeeze the glutes to rise. Hold a plate once 3×12 is easy. |
| 5b | Seated Leg Curl | 3×10-15 | RIR 0-1 | 90 s after the superset | Lean your torso forward a little for a bigger stretch. |
| 5c | Standing Calf Raise | 3×10-15 | RIR 0-1 | 90 s after the superset | 2 s pause in the full stretch at the bottom. |
| 6a | Abs: Dragon Flag Negative | 3×4 (5 s down) | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Grip the bench behind your head, body rigid, lower over 5 s. |
| 6b | Abs: Hollow Body Hold | 3×30 s | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Lower back pressed into the floor, arms and legs long. |

> Exercises with the same number and a letter (4a/4b/4c, 5a/5b/5c) are a **superset**: do one set of each back to back, then rest.

### Week 4: Deload

- **Main lifts:** follow the Week 4 row in the main-lift table.
- **Everything else:** cut sets in half (usually to **2 sets**). Keep the same reps, drop the load 10-15%, and stop at **RIR 4 or more**.
- **Abs:** 2 sets each.
- The point of a deload is to clear fatigue so you come back stronger. Don't skip it, and don't turn it into a hard week.
