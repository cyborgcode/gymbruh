# Month 2: Hypertrophy I

**Weeks 5-8** · Quarter 1: *Build the Base: Foundation & Hypertrophy* · 3 sessions/week

[← Month 1](01-foundation.md) · [Overview](../README.md) · [Month 3 →](03-hypertrophy-2.md)

## Goal

Start the real muscle-building work: more sets on the main lifts and sets taken closer to failure. Week 4 is the first deload.

## This month at a glance

| | Prescription |
|---|---|
| **Main lifts** | See the week-by-week table · rest 2 min |
| **Compound accessories** | 3×10-12 @ RIR 2 · rest 90 s |
| **Isolation** | 3×12-15 @ RIR 2 · rest 60 s |
| **Abs** | 2 exercises every session, done as a superset (see each day) |
| **Cardio** | 30 min before every session: Zone 2 (60-70% HRmax). As you get fitter, raise the incline or resistance so your heart rate stays in the zone. |
| **Intensity technique** | None. Use double progression: add reps each week, then add weight. |
| **Nutrition** | Lean gain: TDEE + 200-300 kcal, protein 1.6-2.2 g/kg. Starting with higher body fat? Eat at maintenance (recomposition). |

## Main lifts: week by week

Applies to all six main lifts: **Bench Press, Overhead Press** (Push), **Barbell Row, Pull-up** (Pull), **Leg Press, Hip Thrust** (Legs).

| Week | Sets × Reps | Load (% of 1RM) | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (wk 5) | 4×8 | ≈ 68-70% | RIR 3 |
| 2 (wk 6) | 4×8 | ≈ 72% | RIR 2 |
| 3 (wk 7) | 4×8 | ≈ 74% | RIR 1-2 |
| 4 (wk 8) | 2×8 | ≈ 60% | RIR 5 (deload) |

> **Load basis:** Months 1-3 go by effort, not percentages. Pick a weight that leaves the target RIR (reps in reserve). The % column is a rough guide only.

## Day C: Legs (quads, glutes, hamstrings, calves)

> Exercises with the same number and a letter (4a/4b/4c, 5a/5b/5c) are a **superset**: do one set of each back to back, then rest.

### Week 4: Deload

- **Main lifts:** follow the Week 4 row in the main-lift table.
- **Everything else:** cut sets in half (usually to **2 sets**). Keep the same reps, drop the load 10-15%, and stop at **RIR 4 or more**.
- **Abs:** 2 sets each.
- **Cardio:** the full 30 min, but easy Zone 2 only.
- The point of a deload is to clear fatigue so you come back stronger. Don't skip it, and don't turn it into a hard week.
