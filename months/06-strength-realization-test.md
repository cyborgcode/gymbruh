# Month 6: Strength III: Realization & Test

**Weeks 22-25** · Quarter 2: *Get Strong: Strength* · 3 sessions/week

[← Month 5](05-strength-intensification.md) · [Overview](../README.md) · [Month 7 →](07-hypertrophy-volume.md)

## Goal

Practise heavy triples, doubles and singles, then test your new maxes in Week 4. The tested numbers set your percentages for the whole of Q3.

## This month at a glance

| | Prescription |
|---|---|
| **Main lifts** | See the week-by-week table · rest 3-4 min |
| **Compound accessories** | 3×6-8 @ RIR 1-2 · rest 2 min |
| **Isolation** | 3×10-12 @ RIR 1 · rest 60-75 s |
| **Abs** | 2 exercises every session, done as a superset (see each day) |
| **Cardio** | 30 min before every session: Zone 2, low-impact only. In test week stay at the easy end (60% HRmax). |
| **Intensity technique** | None. |
| **Nutrition** | Lean gain: TDEE + 200-300 kcal, protein 1.6-2.2 g/kg. Starting with higher body fat? Eat at maintenance (recomposition). |

## Main lifts: week by week

Applies to all six main lifts: **Bench Press, Leg Press, Hip Thrust, Barbell Row, Overhead Press, Pull-up**.

| Week | Sets × Reps | Load (% of 1RM) | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (wk 22) | 1×3 then 3×5 | 87.5% then 77.5% | Top set RPE 8 |
| 2 (wk 23) | 1×2 then 3×4 | 90% then 80% | Top set RPE 8 |

### Week 4: Test week

Do 30 min of easy Zone 2 cardio first, as always, then test:

| Day | Lift 1 | Lift 2 |
|---|---|---|
| A | Bench Press: **1RM** (spotter or safety bars) | Leg Press: **5RM** |
| B | Hip Thrust: **5RM** | Barbell Row: **5RM** (strict, from safeties) |
| C | Overhead Press: **1RM** (or 3RM) | Weighted Pull-up: **3RM** (bodyweight + added) |

- **Warm-up ladder for a 1RM:** empty bar ×10 → 40% ×8 → 60% ×5 → 75% ×3 → 85% ×1 → 90% ×1 → attempt 1 (~95-97%) → attempt 2 (~100-102%) → optional attempt 3 (PR). Rest 3-5 min between heavy attempts.
- **For 3RM/5RM tests:** ramp in 4-6 sets with fewer reps each time, up to a weight you can do for the target reps at RPE 9.5-10.
- Convert every 3RM/5RM to an e1RM with the Epley formula ([README](../README.md#the-maths)).

After testing: **2 sets** of each accessory at RIR 3, and **2 sets** of each abs exercise. Nothing more.

## Flex week (Week 26)

Every quarter ends with one flex week. Choose **one** option:

1. **Deload (recommended):** 3 sessions of 30 min easy Zone 2 cardio, then main lifts **2×5 at 50-60%**, 1 easy set of each accessory, and 2 sets of each abs exercise.
2. **Catch-up:** if you missed sessions this quarter, make them up now.
3. **Full rest or holiday:** no gym, but keep walking 8-10k steps a day.

You just tested. Write down your new 1RMs: they drive Months 7-9.
