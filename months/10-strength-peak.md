# Month 10: Strength Peak & Test

**Weeks 40-43** · Quarter 4: *Peak & Reveal: Strength Peak & Definition* · 3 sessions/week

[← Month 9](09-powerbuilding.md) · [Overview](../README.md) · [Month 11 →](11-definition-1.md)

## Goal

Peak your strength: low reps at high intensity, with accessory volume cut back so you're fresh. Week 4 is test week, aim for PRs on all six lifts.

## This month at a glance

| | Prescription |
|---|---|
| **Main lifts** | See the week-by-week table · rest 3-4 min |
| **Compound accessories** | 3×6-8 @ RIR 2 · rest 2 min |
| **Isolation** | 2×10-12 @ RIR 2 · rest 60 s |
| **Light block** (superset 5) | The muscles you train hard next session: same reps, at most 3 sets, RIR 2-3 |
| **Abs** | 2 exercises every session, done as a superset (see each day) |
| **Warm-up** | 10 min before every session: easy bike or rower, mobility, ramp-up sets ([details](../README.md#warm-up-10-min)) |
| **Intensity technique** | None. Accessory volume is cut back on purpose so you peak. |
| **Nutrition** | Maintenance calories (TDEE), protein 1.6-2.2 g/kg. Fuel the heavy lifting. |

## Main lifts: week by week

Applies to all six main lifts: **Bench Press, Overhead Press** (Push), **Pull-up, Barbell Row** (Pull), **Leg Press, Hip Thrust** (Legs).

| Week | Sets × Reps | Load (% of 1RM) | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (wk 40) | 5×3 | 85% | RIR 2 |
| 2 (wk 41) | 4×2 | 90% | RIR 1-2 |
| 3 (wk 42) | 1×1 then 2×2 | 95% then 85% | Single RPE 8.5-9 |
| 4 (wk 43) | TEST | See test protocol below | PR attempts |

> **Load basis:** Percentages are of the higher of: your Week 25 test, or the e1RM from your Month 9, Week 4 top set.

## Day C: Legs + light Push (quads, glutes, hamstrings, calves · light block: chest, side delts, triceps)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Leg Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 3-4 min | Feet shoulder-width, mid-platform. Lower until your hips are about to lift off the pad. Never lock the knees. |
| 2 | **Barbell Hip Thrust** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 3-4 min | Upper back on the bench, chin tucked, ribs down. Shins vertical at lockout, 1 s squeeze at the top. |
| 3 | Hack Squat | 3×6-8 | RIR 2 | 2 min | A machine, so there's no bar on your back. Feet shoulder-width, lower under control until your thighs pass parallel, then drive up through the whole foot. |

| 4b | Seated Calf Raise | 2×10-12 | RIR 2 | 60 s after the superset | 2 s pause at the bottom, 1 s squeeze at the top. |
| 5a | Light: Machine Chest Press | 3×6-8 | RIR 2-3 | 2 min after the superset | Set the seat so the handles line up with mid-chest. Deep stretch, smooth press. |
| 5b | Light: Dumbbell Lateral Raise | 2×10-12 | RIR 2-3 | 2 min after the superset | Lean forward slightly, pinkies level with thumbs, slow and controlled. |
| 5c | Light: Rope Triceps Pushdown | 2×10-12 | RIR 2-3 | 2 min after the superset | Elbows pinned to your sides, spread the rope at the bottom. |

> Exercises with the same number and a letter (4a/4b, 5a/5b/5c) are a **superset**: do one set of each back to back, then rest. Superset 5 is the **light block**: it trains the muscles you work hard next session, so stop 2-3 reps short.

### Week 4: Test week

Warm up as usual ([README](../README.md#warm-up-10-min)), then test:

| Day | Lift 1 | Lift 2 |
|---|---|---|
| A (Push) | Bench Press: **1RM** (spotter or safety bars) | Overhead Press: **3RM** |
| B (Pull) | Weighted Pull-up: **3RM** (bodyweight + added) | Barbell Row: **5RM** (strict, from safeties) |
| C (Legs) | Leg Press: **5RM** | Hip Thrust: **5RM** |

- **Warm-up ladder for a 1RM:** empty bar ×10 → 40% ×8 → 60% ×5 → 75% ×3 → 85% ×1 → 90% ×1 → attempt 1 (~95-97%) → attempt 2 (~100-102%) → optional attempt 3 (PR). Rest 3-5 min between heavy attempts.
- **For 3RM/5RM tests:** ramp in 4-6 sets with fewer reps each time, up to a weight you can do for the target reps at RPE 9.5-10.
- Convert every 3RM/5RM to an e1RM with the Epley formula ([README](../README.md#the-maths)).

After testing: **2 sets** of each accessory (light block included) at RIR 3, and **2 sets** of each abs exercise. Nothing more.
