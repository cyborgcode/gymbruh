# Month 3: Hypertrophy II

**Weeks 9-12** · Quarter 1: *Build the Base: Foundation & Hypertrophy* · 3 sessions/week

[← Month 2](02-hypertrophy-1.md) · [Overview](../README.md) · [Month 4 →](04-strength-accumulation.md)

## Goal

Heavier hypertrophy work: 6-8 reps on the main lifts and drop sets on isolation work. At the end of the month you estimate your 1RMs, which set your Month 4 percentages.

## This month at a glance

| | Prescription |
|---|---|
| **Main lifts** | See the week-by-week table · rest 2 min |
| **Compound accessories** | 4×8-10 @ RIR 1-2 · rest 90 s |
| **Isolation** | 3×10-15 @ RIR 1 · rest 60 s |
| **Abs** | 2 exercises every session, done as a superset (see each day) |
| **Cardio** | 30 min before every session: Zone 2 (60-70% HRmax). |
| **Intensity technique** | Drop set on the LAST set of every isolation exercise: hit the target reps, drop the weight about 25%, then rep out to RIR 0-1. |
| **Nutrition** | Lean gain: TDEE + 200-300 kcal, protein 1.6-2.2 g/kg. Starting with higher body fat? Eat at maintenance (recomposition). |

## Main lifts: week by week

Applies to all six main lifts: **Bench Press, Leg Press, Hip Thrust, Barbell Row, Overhead Press, Pull-up**.

| Week | Sets × Reps | Load (% of 1RM) | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (wk 9) | 4×8 | ≈ 72% | RIR 2 |
| 2 (wk 10) | 4×7 | ≈ 75% | RIR 2 |
| 3 (wk 11) | 4×6 | ≈ 78% | RIR 1-2 |
| 4 (wk 12) | 4×6 | ≈ 80% | RIR 1, log it for your e1RM |

> **Load basis:** Months 1-3 go by effort, not percentages. Pick a weight that leaves the target RIR (reps in reserve). The % column is a rough guide only. After Week 4, calculate your e1RM for every main lift with the RIR-adjusted Epley formula in the README.

## Day A: Push & Quads

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Barbell Bench Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Shoulder blades squeezed and down, feet planted, bar to mid-chest, press up and slightly back. |
| 2 | **Leg Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Feet shoulder-width, mid-platform. Lower until your hips are about to lift off the pad. Never lock the knees. |
| 3a | Incline Dumbbell Press (30°) | 4×8-10 | RIR 1-2 | 90 s after the superset | Upper-chest builder. Elbows at about 45°, deep stretch at the bottom. |
| 3b | Lat Pulldown (wide grip) | 4×8-10 | RIR 1-2 | 90 s after the superset | Chest up, drive the elbows down to your ribs, control the way up. |
| 4a | Leg Extension | 3×10-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | 1 s squeeze at the top, 3 s on the way down. |
| 4b | Standing Calf Raise | 3×10-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | 2 s pause in the full stretch at the bottom. |
| 5a | Cable Lateral Raise | 3×10-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Lead with the elbow, stop at shoulder height, no swinging. |
| 5b | Rope Triceps Pushdown | 3×10-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Elbows pinned to your sides, spread the rope at the bottom. |
| 6a | Abs: Hanging Knee Raise | 3×15 | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | No swinging. Curl the pelvis up at the top (posterior tilt). |

## Day B: Pull & Posterior Chain

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Barbell Hip Thrust** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Upper back on the bench, chin tucked, ribs down. Shins vertical at lockout, 1 s squeeze at the top. |
| 2 | **Barbell Row** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Unrack from safeties at knee height so you never pull the bar off the floor. Torso 30-45°, brace, pull to the lower ribs. |

| 4a | Single-Arm Dumbbell Row | 4×8-10 | RIR 1-2 | 90 s after the superset | Hand on the bench, pull the elbow toward your hip, full stretch at the bottom. |
| 4b | Lying Leg Curl | 3×10-15 | RIR 1 | 90 s after the superset | Press your hips into the pad, 3 s on the way down. |
| 5a | Face Pull | 3×10-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Pull to your forehead, thumbs back, squeeze the rear delts. |
| 5b | Incline Dumbbell Curl | 3×10-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Arms hang behind the body for a big stretch. No swinging. |

## Day C: Shoulders, Back Width & Arms

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Standing Overhead Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Squeeze glutes and abs, bar travels straight up, push your head through at lockout. |
| 2 | **Pull-up (add weight once 3×10 is easy)** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Dead hang to chin over the bar. Can't hit the reps? Use the assisted machine or a band. |
| 3a | Dips (assisted if needed) | 4×8-10 | RIR 1-2 | 90 s after the superset | Lean forward slightly for chest. Only go as deep as your shoulders are comfortable. |
| 3b | Dumbbell Step-up | 4×8-10 | RIR 1-2 | 90 s after the superset | Knee-height box. Drive through the front heel and don't push off the back foot. |
| 4a | Seated Leg Curl | 3×10-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Lean your torso forward a little for a bigger stretch. |
| 4b | Seated Calf Raise | 3×10-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | 2 s pause at the bottom, 1 s squeeze at the top. |
| 5a | EZ-Bar Curl | 3×10-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Elbows still, no hip swing, 2-3 s on the way down. |
| 5b | Overhead Cable Triceps Extension | 3×10-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Deep stretch behind the head. This trains the long head, the biggest part of the arm. |
| 5c | Dumbbell Lateral Raise | 3×10-15 | RIR 1 | 60 s after the superset | Lean forward slightly, pinkies level with thumbs, slow and controlled. |
| 6a | Abs: Reverse Crunch | 3×15 | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Curl the hips off the bench toward your chest, lower slowly. |
| 6b | Abs: Side Plank | 3×40 s/side | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Straight line from ear to hip to ankle, hips high. |

> Exercises with the same number and a letter (3a/3b, 5a/5b/5c) are a **superset**: do one set of each back to back, then rest.

## Flex week (Week 13)

Every quarter ends with one flex week. Choose **one** option:

1. **Deload (recommended):** 3 sessions of 30 min easy Zone 2 cardio, then main lifts **2×5 at 50-60%**, 1 easy set of each accessory, and 2 sets of each abs exercise.
2. **Catch-up:** if you missed sessions this quarter, make them up now.
3. **Full rest or holiday:** no gym, but keep walking 8-10k steps a day.

Before Month 4, calculate an **e1RM for each main lift** from your Month 3, Week 4 sets (RIR-adjusted Epley).
