# Month 1: Foundation

**Weeks 1-4** · Quarter 1: *Build the Base: Foundation & Hypertrophy* · 3 sessions/week

[Overview](../README.md) · [Month 2 →](02-hypertrophy-1.md)

## Goal

Learn and groove the technique on the six main lifts, build the habit of 3 sessions a week, and start your log. Loads are deliberately light: leave 2-4 reps in the tank on every set.

## This month at a glance

| | Prescription |
|---|---|
| **Main lifts** | See the week-by-week table · rest 2 min |
| **Compound accessories** | 3×12 @ RIR 3 · rest 90 s |
| **Isolation** | 3×12-15 @ RIR 3 · rest 60 s |
| **Abs** | 2 exercises every session, done as a superset (see each day) |
| **Warm-up** | 10 min before every session: easy bike or rower, mobility, ramp-up sets ([details](../README.md#warm-up-10-min)) |
| **Intensity technique** | None yet. Own the technique: lower every rep over 2-3 s. |
| **Nutrition** | Lean gain: TDEE + 200-300 kcal, protein 1.6-2.2 g/kg. Starting with higher body fat? Eat at maintenance (recomposition). |

## Main lifts: week by week

Applies to all six main lifts: **Bench Press, Overhead Press** (Push), **Barbell Row, Pull-up** (Pull), **Leg Press, Hip Thrust** (Legs).

| Week | Sets × Reps | Load (% of 1RM) | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (wk 1) | 3×10 | ≈ 60-65% | RIR 4 |
| 2 (wk 2) | 3×10 | ≈ 65% | RIR 3 |
| 3 (wk 3) | 3×10 | ≈ 67-70% | RIR 3 |
| 4 (wk 4) | 3×10 | ≈ 70% | RIR 2 |

> **Load basis:** Months 1-3 go by effort, not percentages. Pick a weight that leaves the target RIR (reps in reserve). The % column is a rough guide only.

## Day A: Push (chest, shoulders, triceps)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Barbell Bench Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Shoulder blades squeezed and down, feet planted, bar to mid-chest, press up and slightly back. |
| 2 | **Standing Overhead Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Squeeze glutes and abs, bar travels straight up, push your head through at lockout. |
| 3 | Incline Dumbbell Press (30°) | 3×12 | RIR 3 | 90 s | Upper-chest builder. Elbows at about 45°, deep stretch at the bottom. |
| 4a | Cable Lateral Raise | 3×12-15 | RIR 3 | 60 s after the superset | Lead with the elbow, stop at shoulder height, no swinging. |
| 4b | Rope Triceps Pushdown | 3×12-15 | RIR 3 | 60 s after the superset | Elbows pinned to your sides, spread the rope at the bottom. |
| 5a | Pec Deck Fly | 3×12-15 | RIR 3 | 60 s after the superset | Slight bend in the elbows, deep stretch, squeeze 1 s with the hands together. |
| 5b | Overhead Cable Triceps Extension | 3×12-15 | RIR 3 | 60 s after the superset | Deep stretch behind the head. This trains the long head, the biggest part of the arm. |
| 6a | Abs: Hanging Knee Raise | 3×10 | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | No swinging. Curl the pelvis up at the top (posterior tilt). |
| 6b | Abs: Cable Crunch | 3×12 | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Round the spine and bring the ribs to the pelvis. Hips stay still. |

## Day B: Pull (back, rear delts, biceps)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Barbell Row** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Unrack from safeties at knee height so you never pull the bar off the floor. Torso 30-45°, brace, pull to the lower ribs. |
| 2 | **Pull-up (add weight once 3×10 is easy)** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Dead hang to chin over the bar. Can't hit the reps? Use the assisted machine or a band. |
| 3 | Lat Pulldown (wide grip) | 3×12 | RIR 3 | 90 s | Chest up, drive the elbows down to your ribs, control the way up. |
| 4a | Face Pull | 3×12-15 | RIR 3 | 60 s after the superset | Pull to your forehead, thumbs back, squeeze the rear delts. |
| 4b | Incline Dumbbell Curl | 3×12-15 | RIR 3 | 60 s after the superset | Arms hang behind the body for a big stretch. No swinging. |
| 5a | Dumbbell Lateral Raise | 3×12-15 | RIR 3 | 60 s after the superset | Lean forward slightly, pinkies level with thumbs, slow and controlled. |
| 5b | Hammer Curl | 3×12-15 | RIR 3 | 60 s after the superset | Neutral grip trains the brachialis for thicker arms. |
| 6a | Abs: Kneeling Ab Wheel Rollout | 3×6-8 (partial range) | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Ribs down, glutes tight. Roll out only as far as your lower back stays flat. |
| 6b | Abs: Pallof Press | 3×10/side | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Stand side-on to the cable, press out and hold 2 s. Don't let it twist you. |

## Day C: Legs (quads, glutes, hamstrings, calves)

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Effort | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Leg Press** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Feet shoulder-width, mid-platform. Lower until your hips are about to lift off the pad. Never lock the knees. |
| 2 | **Barbell Hip Thrust** (main) | Main-lift table | Main-lift table | 2 min | Upper back on the bench, chin tucked, ribs down. Shins vertical at lockout, 1 s squeeze at the top. |
| 3 | Dumbbell Walking Lunge | 3×12 | RIR 3 | 90 s | Long stride, slight forward lean, back knee lightly touches the floor. |
| 4a | Leg Extension | 3×12-15 | RIR 3 | 60 s after the superset | 1 s squeeze at the top, 3 s on the way down. |
| 4b | Lying Leg Curl | 3×12-15 | RIR 3 | 60 s after the superset | Press your hips into the pad, 3 s on the way down. |
| 4c | Standing Calf Raise | 3×12-15 | RIR 3 | 60 s after the superset | 2 s pause in the full stretch at the bottom. |
| 5a | 45° Back Extension | 3×12 | RIR 3 | 90 s after the superset | Round slightly over the pad and squeeze the glutes to rise. Hold a plate once 3×12 is easy. |
| 5b | Seated Leg Curl | 3×12-15 | RIR 3 | 90 s after the superset | Lean your torso forward a little for a bigger stretch. |
| 5c | Seated Calf Raise | 3×12-15 | RIR 3 | 90 s after the superset | 2 s pause at the bottom, 1 s squeeze at the top. |
| 6a | Abs: Reverse Crunch | 3×10 | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Curl the hips off the bench toward your chest, lower slowly. |
| 6b | Abs: Side Plank | 3×20 s/side | Strict; stop 1-2 reps (or 5-10 s) short of failure | 45-60 s after the superset | Straight line from ear to hip to ankle, hips high. |

> Exercises with the same number and a letter (4a/4b/4c, 5a/5b/5c) are a **superset**: do one set of each back to back, then rest.
